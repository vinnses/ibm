## Coverage and segmentation

The proposals yield 68 source-level candidate topic records across broad working domains: Fundamentos de Computação, Programação e Algoritmos, Estruturas de Dados, Teoria da Computação, Redes e Sistemas Distribuídos, Bioquímica, Biologia Molecular, Bioinformática, and Estatística. These are emergent organizational labels, not an official taxonomy.

The source corpus is concentrated in four computing components plus three external-department components. It does not cover all 37 formal components of curriculum 2011, does not establish offering-wide content, and must not support negative claims such as absence or removal.

## Ambiguities retained

- A code and component title were not used to establish applicability. The source may be linked later to the formal `curriculum-2011-<code>` component instance only with its documentary limitation preserved.
- Ficha 2 records have not been merged into their Ficha 1 counterparts. CI055, CI057, and CI244 Ficha 2 records explicitly identify Ciência da Computação; CI056 Ficha 2 identifies Informática Biomédica but has no 96A version statement.
- The 2022 BQ005, BQ054, and CE003 records are included because their source paths sit in the historical inventory, but their date/application limitation is explicit. They are not treated as historical 2011 descriptions.
- CI057's phrase `Conjuntos não ordenáveis` is not proposed as a topic: it lacks enough context to choose a stable intermediate label. `Heap` is kept as a separate, narrowly named proposal rather than treated as an algorithm or priority queue.
- `Estilos de programação`, `Características básicas do computador`, and general statements of algorithms were not inflated into broad, unsupported taxonomic claims.

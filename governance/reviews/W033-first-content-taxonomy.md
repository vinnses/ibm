## Qualitative sample

- Simple continuity: exact source-grounded overlap for `algoritmos de busca`
  remains a proposed `maintained` candidate, not a formal continuity fact.
- New: `autenticação e controle de acesso` is `new` only relative to the
  explicitly sparse usable 2011 corpus; its strength is indeterminate.
- Possible reduction: the initial recurrence-relation candidate failed audit
  because CI1056 Ficha 2 explicitly contains recurrence relations. It was
  removed rather than weakened rhetorically.
- Split: one combined 2011 search/sorting topic maps to two 2023 topic units as
  a proposed `fragmented` candidate.
- Merge: two 2011 linear-structure units map to a broader 2023 basic-structures
  unit as a proposed `merged` hypothesis.
- Indeterminate: the networks case remains indeterminate because insufficient
  2023 corpus coverage cannot support removal.

## Limits and scope

Only 9 of 12 2011-side source records are occurrence-eligible, covering seven
component instances; all applicability remains indeterminate. The 40
2023-side records cover 24 component instances and likewise do not prove
application to the 2023 curriculum. The taxonomy is emergent and unreviewed,
not official. No full taxonomy, human review completion, semantic similarity,
2026 evaluation, argument, narrative, or final design was produced.

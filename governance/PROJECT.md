# Project Charter

## Purpose

Build a verifiable documentary, curricular, and administrative history of the UFPR Bachelor of Biomedical Informatics, its curriculum reforms, and the 2026 reorganization proposal.

The documentary record must be sufficiently reconstructed before the project evaluates curricular quality, compares alternatives, or proposes a future course.

Immediate user priority, 2026-09-05: deliver relevant stable data and original documents in small committed batches. Follow `governance/DATA_FIRST.md`. Comparative analysis, evaluative synthesis and checks of current operational compliance/offering are deferred. P1 inventory closure does not mean all underlying data have been obtained.

## Evidence model

Every substantive statement must be classified as one of:

- **Direct fact:** explicitly supported by a preserved source.
- **Inference:** derived from stated evidence and method.
- **Interpretation:** analytical reading that may admit alternatives.
- **Proposal or normative judgment:** recommendation or value judgment.

Uncertainty should use these labels when useful:

- **proven** — supported by adequate preserved evidence;
- **probable** — supported indirectly but not established completely;
- **contradictory** — sources conflict or a claim conflicts with official evidence;
- **not located** — a bounded search did not find the evidence.

“Not located” never means “does not exist.”

## Historical integrity

- Reconstruct each curriculum as its own historical unit.
- Do not assign a current Ficha, department, requirement, or equivalence to an earlier curriculum without documentary support.
- Preserve historical names, codes, dates, versions, and disagreements.
- Distinguish permanent Ficha 1 content from variable Ficha 2 offerings.
- Do not merge multiple Ficha 2 documents into a synthetic plan.

## Administrative status vocabulary

Use the following states only when separately evidenced:

1. `proposal`
2. `selected`
3. `approved`
4. `authorized`
5. `implemented`

The 2026 course document is currently established only as a located proposal. No public evidence in the repository establishes later states.

## Quantitative integrity

- Annual snapshots and cumulative cohort indicators answer different questions and must remain separate.
- Keep UFPR/SIGA and INEP values side by side when their universes differ.
- Do not estimate absolute applicants from a candidate-per-vacancy ratio and a vacancy count from another source.
- Do not call entrants divided by nominal vacancies “final occupancy.”
- Report denominator, cohort, reference year, follow-up duration, and source method.

## Deliverable standard

A research milestone is complete only when its conclusions trace to preserved evidence, manifests are valid, gaps and conflicts are explicit, work-specific checks pass, commits are scoped, and a handoff records what was and was not done.

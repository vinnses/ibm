# Human review questions — W010 2023 curriculum inventory

## HR-W010-001 — Current/applicable Ficha 1 access

- **Related gap:** many 2023 components lack a publicly located Ficha 1 whose version is demonstrably applicable; several preserved Fichas predate the reform.
- **Why agent work is insufficient:** public code/title matches do not prove applicability, and approved departmental versions may be in internal systems.
- **Human action/question:** can an authorized UFPR custodian provide the approved Ficha 1 for each 2023 component with approval date, department, and validity/applicability statement?
- **Required response provenance:** original bytes, custodian, request/response date, stated version/validity, and disclosure limits.
- **Gate consequence:** public-source statuses can remain indeterminate, but missing applicable Fichas materially limit P2 reconciliation and P3 content comparison.
- **Status:** pending human access decision.

## HR-W010-002 — Term/class-specific Ficha 2 access for 2023-2026

- **Related gap:** no complete public set of 2023-2026 Ficha 2 documents identified by term and class.
- **Why agent work is insufficient:** plans may exist only in authenticated teaching/departmental repositories.
- **Human action/question:** can authorized course/department staff export Ficha 2 records with code, class, term, instructor, approval/status, and original file for prioritized offerings?
- **Required response provenance:** keep every version separate and record term/class; do not synthesize plans.
- **Gate consequence:** not proof of nonexistence and not necessarily blocking the formal 2023 matrix; material limitation for program-level P3 comparison.
- **Status:** pending human access decision.

## HR-W010-003 — Authoritative status of Portal do Ementário

- **Related gap:** the relationship between the resolution/PPC and the portal representation is stale or uncertain, and W010 initially preserved the wrong page for the claim.
- **Why agent work is insufficient:** only the responsible UFPR unit can confirm whether a portal record is archival, current, superseded, or a publication defect.
- **Human action/question:** which curriculum/version does Ementário record `v=1225` officially represent, and what is the administrative explanation for any 3,000/3,200-hour divergence?
- **Required response provenance:** dated response from the responsible unit plus the exact captured portal response.
- **Gate consequence:** agent must first correct provenance; human confirmation remains a P2 reconciliation input.
- **Status:** pending human clarification; not a substitute for the agent correction.

# Work Specification — W009: Complete the 2011 documentary inventory

- Objective: produce a per-component documentary inventory for the 2011 curriculum covering all 37 coded components and four elective spaces, with preserved evidence, explicit applicability/version judgments, transcribed formal electives and ementas, and structured dependencies.
- Branch: `work/w009-p1-curriculum-2011`
- Commit base: `22b14805956fcece4e381dc089f7dbb06d2b0857`
- Error log: `governance/errors/W009.md`
- Human review: `governance/human-reviews/W009-p1-curriculum-2011.md`
- Inputs: preserved 2010 PPC and Resolução nº 34/2010-CEPE; captured 2011 curriculum pages; official public UFPR/CEPE/SOC/DInf/department/Ementário sources; repository documentary method and source specification.
- In scope: transcribe the formal elective catalog; enumerate the 37 coded components and four elective spaces; locate and preserve applicable Ficha 1 documents or record bounded negative searches; transcribe ementas with source/version/applicability; locate and preserve Ficha 2 documents by term/class without merging versions; validate offering departments, prerequisites, correquisites, equivalences, and hidden formal requirements; create structured component and dependency datasets; document every conflict and indeterminate assignment.
- Out of scope: 2023 inventory; administrative/procedural history; curricular quality judgment; cross-curriculum comparison; proposal design; inferring historical content or applicability from a current code/title alone.
- Deliverables: `curriculos/2011/inventario/componentes.csv`, `optativas.csv`, `ementas.csv`, `dependencias.csv`, and `buscas-negativas.csv`; `curriculos/2011/fichas/manifesto.csv` and preserved originals organized by document type/version; `curriculos/2011/fontes/manifesto.csv`; explanatory README; W009 handoff. Review is performed cross-branch after collection.
- Method: treat 2011 as an independent historical unit; begin from formal acts; preserve every used public source unchanged before extraction; hash with SHA-256; distinguish Ficha 1 from each Ficha 2; establish applicability from dated/versioned evidence rather than code continuity; encode fact/inference and proven/probable/contradictory/not-located states; log negative searches with date, domains, terms, results, and limits.
- Acceptance criteria: exactly 41 inventory targets (37 coded components plus four elective spaces) each have status, evidence path or bounded-search record, source hash where preserved, version/applicability assessment, department/prerequisite/correquisite/equivalence/hidden-requirement state, and explicit gaps/conflicts; the formal elective catalog and available ementas are transcribed against preserved sources; dependency data reproduce formal requirements; every Ficha 2 remains individually versioned; work-specific and repository validation pass.
- Risks and uncertainty: historical Ementário/Fichas may be inaccessible, undated, replaced, or available only behind authentication; later sources may document equivalence without proving 2011 applicability; formal sources may conflict. A protected required source or materially ambiguous interpretation triggers a blocked handoff rather than a guess.
- Validation: component/elective target counts; unique stable identifiers; manifest path/hash/status checks; source-to-transcription sampling for every source/version; dependency endpoint and cycle checks with documented legitimate cycles if any; Ficha type/version checks; CSV width checks; `python scripts/validate_repository.py`; `git diff --check`; cross-review under `governance/specs/REVIEW.md`.

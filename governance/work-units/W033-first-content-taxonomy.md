- **Method:** consolidate literal/normalized ementa and program text without modifying originals; retain each document version separately; record curriculum, component, document kind, applicability, status, path, hash, URL, and locator; propose intermediate-granularity topics only from quoted support; keep automated records `proposed`; use aliases only with recorded justification; keep evidence strength independent from change type; prefer `indeterminate` over unsupported removal; represent split/merge through arrays of source and target occurrences; record any lexical heuristic inputs/scores as candidate metadata, never truth.
- **Acceptance criteria:** a usable emergent taxonomy covers every source with usable content in the declared corpus; every occurrence resolves to evidence and a document; uncertain applicability propagates to occurrence epistemic state/notes; every topic has one domain; alias references are acyclic and justified; stable IDs and all references validate; lineage candidates have valid endpoints/evidence/justification and no unsupported removal; site supports domain/topic navigation, curriculum filtering, evidence/document access, review states, and candidate inspection; qualitative examples cover maintained/new/reduced/split/merge/indeterminate or explicitly document why a class is not supportable; Docker/site and repository suites pass.
- **Risks and uncertainty:** 2011 content coverage is sparse and often not proven applicable to curriculum 96A; many 2023 Fichas predate the reform and remain applicability-indeterminate; Ficha 2 is offering-specific; external-department coverage is uneven; source terminology may support several reasonable granularities; “new” and “removed” are especially coverage-sensitive.
- **Validation:** deterministic rebuild/check; schema and semantic invariants; source file/hash/locator checks; occurrence/evidence/applicability propagation; aliases and relation endpoints; explicit removal justification; regression tests; qualitative sample audit; `docker compose config`, `docker compose build`, route/runtime checks, W031/W032/repository/governance validators, dependency audit, secret/public-tree audit, and `git diff --check`.
- **Error log:** `governance/errors/W033.md` (append-only).
- **Human review:** no blocking review is anticipated; generated topics/occurrences/relations remain `proposed` for site-based review. Material ambiguity requiring authority or testimony will receive a separate W033 human-review record.

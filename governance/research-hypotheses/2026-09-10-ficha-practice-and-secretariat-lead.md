## Statement preserved in substance

The stakeholder states that TCC components do not have Fichas because they are conclusion-of-course work; that the department treats Ficha 1 as generic and Ficha 2 according to the student's work; that Ficha 2 documents are useful for determining what is actually taught while Ficha 1 formally defines the course; and that the course secretariat is expected to have a folder remembered as `emendas,fichas1,fichas2`.

The stakeholder also directs that similar-name/different-code BCC components be shown for DInf targets, but that the same exercise not be projected onto Biological Sciences or Health Sciences because their components are generally adapted for Biomedical Informatics.

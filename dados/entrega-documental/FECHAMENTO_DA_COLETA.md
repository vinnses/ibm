The documentary state remains `proposal`. Proposed matrix/PPC/equivalences, process linkage, decisions, staffing guarantees and later administrative acts are not established by that form.

## Remaining gaps and their causes

The complete [W030 classification](../../governance/reviews/W030-gap-classification.csv) and [evidence audit](../../governance/reviews/W030-gap-audit.md) retain original gap IDs and distinguish:

- `closed`: sufficient structured evidence for the specified factual scope; not exhaustive institutional recovery.
- `deferred_access`: historical/current Ficha applicability, missing term/class plans, historical offering units, authoritative portal status, protected 2026 process records and matched occupancy/cutoff universes require custodians or unavailable access. The [human-review requests](SOLICITACOES_E_LACUNAS.md) state required provenance and gate consequences.
- `not_located_public_search_exhausted`: original Resolução 19/10-COUN, original DOU facsimile of Portaria 44/2015, course-specific Enade/e-MEC details, missing applicant totals and previously searched Ficha targets retain their bounded search outcomes. Institutional reproduction and registry summaries are not substituted for originals.
- `new_lead_required`: further public retrieval needs a concrete unexamined document, address, archive location or other new source clue. A still-missing value alone is not a new lead.
- `out_of_scope_for_data_phase`: curricular comparison, similarity, lineage, interpretation, evaluation, argument and visualization are not collection tasks.

No new public search, download or institutional contact occurred in W030. No new external lead was identified. A custodian-supplied original or new precise lead may support a separately authorized intake Work; this closure does not assert nonexistence of missing documents.

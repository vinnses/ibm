# Documentary axis: 2011 curriculum

## Scope and status

This axis covers the formal 2011 curriculum as reconstructed from the preserved resolution and PPC, the structured inventories, the preserved Ementário pages, and bounded Ficha searches. It is a navigation aid, not a claim that every historical teaching document was obtained or that every current code is applicable to 2011.

Uncertainty labels follow the project charter: `proven`, `probable`, `contradictory`, and `not located`. The Ficha search records preserve bounded public results; `not located` does not mean that a document does not exist.

## Formal curriculum and structured datasets

- [Component inventory](../../../curriculos/2011/inventario/componentes.csv) — formal component rows, codes, periods and workloads.
- [Dependency inventory](../../../curriculos/2011/inventario/dependencias.csv) — recorded prerequisites, correquisites and related dependency fields; do not add inferred edges.
- [Ementa inventory](../../../curriculos/2011/inventario/ementas.csv) — transcribed/structured ementa fields with their source boundary.
- [Elective inventory](../../../curriculos/2011/inventario/optativas.csv) — four elective spaces and their recorded formal information.
- [Divergence register](../../../curriculos/2011/inventario/divergencias.csv) — conflicts and limitations retained without silent adjudication.
- [Bounded search register](../../../curriculos/2011/inventario/buscas-negativas.csv) — search terms, domains, dates, results and limits.

- [2011 Ficha manifest](../../../curriculos/2011/fichas/manifesto.csv) and [W022–W024 search/manifests](../../../curriculos/2011/fichas/) — Ficha records and bounded searches, kept by work and target.
- [Preserved Ementário course pages](../../../curriculos/2011/fontes/ementario/) — historical page captures; applicability and historical offering-unit status remain qualified.

## Evidence boundaries

The formal matrix, periodization, workload, elective spaces and recorded dependencies are directly navigable through the structured inventories and the preserved resolution/PPC. Ementário pages are preserved representations and must not silently override the normative documents. No Ficha 1 was proven applicable to the 2011 curriculum, and no term/class-specific 2011 Ficha 2 was located in the bounded public search. The applicable-Ficha and historical-offering-unit questions remain in [W009 human review](../../../governance/human-reviews/W009-p1-curriculum-2011.md).

Do not use this axis to infer historical content from a later Ficha, to merge Ficha 2 versions, or to treat a current page as proof of a 2011 offering. For cross-axis use, preserve the source path, document type, date/version and applicability assessment.

# Documentary axis: 2023 curriculum

## Scope and status

This axis covers the formal 2023 curriculum, its preserved resolutions and PPC, structured inventories, separately preserved Ficha 1/Ficha 2 records, and bounded applicability searches. It does not certify that every Ficha is applicable to the 2023 curriculum and does not describe current offering or compliance.

Uncertainty labels follow the project charter: `proven`, `probable`, `contradictory`, and `not located`. A bounded public gap remains a gap; it is not evidence of nonexistence.

## Evidence boundaries

The formal matrix, electives, dependencies and five regulatory subjects are navigable through the structured inventories and preserved resolutions/PPC. Ficha 1 and Ficha 2 remain distinct document types, and multiple Ficha 2 versions are not merged. A code/title match does not prove applicability to the 2023 curriculum. The unresolved applicable-Ficha, term/class-specific Ficha 2 and authoritative Ementário questions are recorded in [W010 human review](../../../governance/human-reviews/W010-p1-curriculum-2023.md).

The preserved Ementário response is a source representation with a documented stale/uncertain relationship to the 2023 formal curriculum; it is not a replacement for the resolutions or PPC. This axis must not be used to infer current offering, current compliance or historical continuity from a later Ficha.

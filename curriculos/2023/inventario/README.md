# W010 inventory — 2023 curriculum

`componentes.csv` has exactly 43 targets: 39 non-TCC components and the four distinct TCC alternatives. `status` describes the documentary state, not a claim that a missing public document does not exist. The 2022 component-creation acts establish the listed new components and departments, but are not Ficha 1 documents.

`ementas.csv` deliberately retains a separate row per preserved Ficha version. DInf Ficha 2 files are also retained separately in `fichas/manifesto-dinf.csv`; their filenames and index entries do not establish a specific term/class, so this collection does not recast them as 2023–2026 plans. No pre-2023 Ficha is treated as applicable on code/title continuity alone.

`optativas.csv` is a complete 92-row literal transcription of the formal catalog in Resolution 75/22-CEPE, Annex I; it does not report offerings. Its `title` field retains the resolution's capitalization, spelling, word sequence, diacritics, hyphens, punctuation, and numerals; its `total_hours` field is the Annex I total column. `dependencias.csv` stores direct endpoints where the resolution has an individual condition. The repeated 17-component barrier is retained literally in the resolution and described in the component records; it is not expanded into edges to avoid obscuring its conjunctive semantics.

The preserved Ementário response at `curriculo.action?v=1225` identifies itself as “Informática Biomédica - 2011 - Corrente” and displays 3,000 hours. It is therefore a portal representation of the earlier curriculum, whereas Resolution 75/22 fixes 3,200 hours for entrants from 2022/2023. The separately preserved `pagina-grade-semestral.html` is a 2025/2 schedule page, not Ementário evidence.

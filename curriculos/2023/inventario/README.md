# W010 inventory — 2023 curriculum

`componentes.csv` has exactly 43 targets: 39 non-TCC components and the four distinct TCC alternatives. `status` describes the documentary state, not a claim that a missing public document does not exist. The 2022 component-creation acts establish the listed new components and departments, but are not Ficha 1 documents.

`ementas.csv` deliberately retains a separate row per preserved Ficha version. DInf Ficha 2 files are also retained separately in `fichas/manifesto-dinf.csv`; their filenames and index entries do not establish a specific term/class, so this collection does not recast them as 2023–2026 plans. No pre-2023 Ficha is treated as applicable on code/title continuity alone.

`optativas.csv` is a transcription of the formal catalog in the resolution; it does not report offerings. `dependencias.csv` stores direct endpoints where the resolution has an individual condition. The repeated 17-component barrier is retained literally in the resolution and described in the component records; it is not expanded into edges to avoid obscuring its conjunctive semantics.

The preserved Ementário page is descriptively conflicting: it presents a 3,000-hour previous structure, whereas Resolution 75/22 fixes the 3,200-hour curriculum for entrants from 2022/2023. It remains a preserved source and is not silently corrected.
